# Code of Conduct

VeriRepro contributors are expected to keep technical collaboration professional, evidence-based, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code, claims, experiments, and evidence rather than people.
- Be explicit about uncertainty and reproducibility limitations.
- Do not pressure maintainers or contributors to disclose private papers, datasets, credentials, identities, or unpublished results.
- Treat security reports and possible sandbox escapes responsibly.
- Prefer reproducible examples and concrete tests over status, authority, or rhetoric.
- Welcome contributors with different levels of experience while maintaining the same technical and safety standards for all changes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private information, credential exposure, or knowingly publishing exploit details before a reasonable private remediation window are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in blocking from the project.

Security-sensitive reports should use the private reporting path described in `SECURITY.md`; do not disclose working exploit details in a public issue.
